## Brand & Style

The design system establishes the visual voice for a premier Indonesian enterprise software house founded in 2010. It projects authority, technical rigor, and vibrant kinetic energy. The aesthetic translates physical high-grade corporate print literature into a precise, responsive digital medium.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Kinetic
The system fuses structured Swiss-style corporate information architecture with energetic diagonal geometry inspired by the Durio Indigo physical brand brochure. It eschews generic soft SaaS tropes in favor of sharp structural divisions, bold color blocking, and purposeful asymmetric accents.

### Core Visual Motifs
- **The Chevron Cut:** A bold, signature 12-degree to 15-degree diagonal panel or clip-path division slicing through hero banners, card headers, and thematic transitions.
- **Micro-Tracked Numerals & Eyebrows:** Editorial mono-spaced index markers (e.g., `01 — LAYANAN`, `02 — PORTFOLIO`) set in heavy uppercase with generous letter tracking.
- **Crisp Structural Discipline:** Generous whitespace anchored by razor-thin borders, high-contrast block backgrounds, and purposeful enterprise status accents.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep navy with energetic high-contrast focal points, rooted in the legacy company profile print brochure.

### Palette Roles & Usage Ratios
- **Deep Navy (`#1B2150` — Primary / 40% Visual Weight):** Anchors main navigations, display hero slices, rich content footers, and foundational headlines.
- **Vibrant Golden Yellow (`#F2C200` — Secondary / 20% Visual Weight):** Applied to diagonal chevron trims, category pill badges, prominent call-to-action highlights, and critical metric milestones.
- **Ribbon Sky Blue (`#29A8E0` — Tertiary / 10% Visual Weight):** Extracted from the kinetic ribbon identity; used for active state indicators, secondary links, and digital capability tags.
- **Obsidian Dark (`#0B0F14` — Base Dark Neutral):** Used for maximum-contrast deep tech sections, code blocks, and primary text on light canvases.
- **Canvas Neutrals:**
  - Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`): Primary light container and editorial canvas.
  - Mist Grey (`#F4F5F7`): Alternating section backdrop, tabular rows, and subtle card surfaces.
- **Enterprise Semantic Accent:**
  - Growth Green (`#2E9E4F`): Direct functional triggers, successful deployment status badges, and verified client deliverables.

## Typography

The typography leverages `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all roles to convey modern Indonesian software engineering prowess. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines & Display:** Set tight with negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to mirror authoritative offset brochure headers.
- **Section Prefixes (`category-eyebrow`):** Strict uppercase treatment with wide tracking (`0.14em`) paired with leading index numerals (e.g., `03 — SOLUSI DIGITAL`) to structure document flow.
- **Body Copy:** Set with generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) to maintain legibility across dual-column white and slate surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts the geometric grid of multi-page technical brochures to modern screens using a 12-column responsive fluid grid.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max layout width capped at `1280px` for structured readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Multi-column brochure rows stack into linear narrative cards.

### The Chevron Section Divider Technique
Section breaks leverage an angled CSS clip-path or SVG mask (`clip-path: polygon(0 0, 100% 0, 100% calc(100% - 3vw), 0 100%)`) to create the physical brochure's trademark dynamic wedge separator between Navy and Light surfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating cloud shadows in favor of architectural depth, crisp planar stratification, and hairline borders.

### Depth Stratification Principles
- **Base Planar Contrast:** Depth is established through deliberate alternating background fills (`#1B2150` Navy, `#0B0F14` Dark, and `#F4F5F7` Mist Grey).
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards and surfaces use a 1px solid border (`rgba(27, 33, 80, 0.08)` on light mode, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` on dark Navy mode).
- **Hard Technical Shadows:** Elevated interactive components utilize tight, calibrated ambient drops with zero blur bleeding:
  - **Level 1 (Resting Card):** `0px 2px 4px rgba(11, 15, 20, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(11, 15, 20, 0.02)`
  - **Level 2 (Hover / Active Overlay):** `0px 10px 20px -5px rgba(27, 33, 80, 0.12), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(27, 33, 80, 0.04)`
  - **Chevron Accent Shadow:** Golden Yellow interactive buttons take an energetic, solid 3px drop offset: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 0px #C9A100`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a deliberate "Soft Structural" profile (`roundedness: 1`), keeping corner radii small and disciplined (`4px` base, `8px` container) to honor engineering precision while avoiding harsh industrial brutalism.

### Corner Treatment
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Interactive Badges:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Display Cards, Dialogs, & Device Enclosures:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Category tag labels and metric counters use full rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to contrast against rectangular grid modules.
- **Diagonal Trims:** Outer banner containers utilize 12-degree to 15-degree clipped corner notches on upper-right or lower-left axes reflecting the chevron brand motif.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic (Brochure CTA):** Background in Vibrant Golden Yellow (`#F2C200`), text in Obsidian Dark (`#0B0F14`), font weight `700`, uppercase, `4px` radius. Hover shifts to `#DDAF00` with subtle `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Corporate Enterprise (Action / Verification):** Background in Enterprise Green (`#2E9E4F`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Used for high-intent conversion actions and project milestones.
- **Navy Secondary:** Background in Deep Navy (`#1B2150`), text in White, with crisp hairline border.
- **Ghost / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1.5px border in `#1B2150` on light backgrounds, or `#29A8E0` on dark sections.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Set in `label-sm` with full uppercase tracking (`0.06em`).
- **Index Tag:** Yellow fill (`#F2C200`) with Navy text, accompanying Roman or numerical taxonomy markers (e.g., `EST. 2010`).
- **Tech Stack Capsule:** Light grey fill (`#F4F5F7`), 1px border in `rgba(27, 33, 80, 0.1)`, Cyan/Sky Blue dot indicator (`#29A8E0`).

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Light Theme Variant:** Crisp White fill with `1px` border in `rgba(27, 33, 80, 0.08)`. Features a 4px high Golden Yellow top-border accent on hover.
- **Navy Kinetic Variant:** Solid `#1B2150` fill with 1px border in `rgba(41, 168, 224, 0.25)`. Headings in Crisp White, sub-labels in `#F2C200`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background in `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px` solid `rgba(27, 33, 80, 0.16)`, `4px` corner radius.
- Focused state transitions to border `#1B2150` with an outer ring of `3px rgba(41, 168, 224, 0.25)`. No muddy drop shadows.

### Realistic Device Frames & Brochure Showcases
- Portfolio mockups must be framed inside realistic flat hardware frames (macOS dark titanium window frames or minimal matte smartphone bezels) resting on Mist Grey canvas pods with 1px hairline boundary rules.
